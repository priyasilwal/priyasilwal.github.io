Environmental Science & Geospatial Research

Priya Silwal

M.S. Candidate in Environmental Science
California State University, Dominguez Hills

I am an environmental science researcher with interests in land-use change, sustainable transportation, environmental monitoring, and water resources. My work combines geographic information systems (GIS), field observations, and statistical analysis to examine environmental patterns and support evidence-based planning and decision-making.

At CSUDH, my graduate research explores how changes in land use relate to vehicle miles traveled across California. I also work on community-based air-quality monitoring and bring previous field experience in groundwater, surface-water, and hydrometeorological studies in Nepal.

Research

Land Use, Transportation & Climate Policy

Examining spatial and temporal relationships between land-use patterns and residential vehicle miles traveled in California. My master's thesis uses GIS and linear mixed-effects modeling to study variation across 18 metropolitan planning organizations and inform sustainable land-use and transportation planning.

Community Air Quality & Environmental Justice

Supporting community-focused air-quality research in West Carson, California, through PurpleAir sensor monitoring at six locations. This work investigates spatial and temporal pollution patterns and incorporates community perspectives into environmental assessment and redevelopment discussions.

Water Resources & Hydrometeorological Monitoring

Studying rainfall, streamflow, groundwater, surface-water quality, and river sediments through field monitoring and data analysis. My previous research in Nepal involved environmental sampling, hydrologic datasets, citizen science, and technical reporting.
