Environmental Science & Geospatial Research

Priya Silwal

M.S. Candidate in Environmental Science
California State University, Dominguez Hills

I study how land-use change, transportation, and environmental conditions affect communities. My research combines geographic information systems (GIS), statistical analysis, and field monitoring to examine sustainable transportation, air quality, and water resources.

Research

Land Use and Sustainable Transportation

Examining the relationship between land-use patterns and residential vehicle miles traveled across 18 California metropolitan planning organizations using GIS and statistical modeling.

Community Air-Quality Monitoring

Analyzing PurpleAir monitoring data across six locations in West Carson, California, to understand local air-quality patterns and support community-based environmental assessment.

Water Resources and Hydrometeorology

Drawing on field experience in Nepal with streamflow, groundwater, water-quality sampling, rainfall analysis, and collaborative environmental monitoring programs.
