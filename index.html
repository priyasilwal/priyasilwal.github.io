A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Where research meets real-world impact.

I am an environmental science researcher and M.S. candidate at California State University, Dominguez Hills. My work brings together geospatial analysis, statistical modeling, field observations, and environmental policy to better understand the places where we live.

Before graduate research in California, I worked in Nepal on surface-water and groundwater monitoring, hydrometeorological data collection, and community-based field projects. Today, I am especially interested in environmental consulting, sustainable transportation, community air quality, and water resources.
